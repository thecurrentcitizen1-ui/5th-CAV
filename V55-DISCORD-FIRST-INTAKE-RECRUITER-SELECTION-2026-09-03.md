- New-arrival messaging now directs recruits to `/link-game` and `/apply` inside Discord.
- `/link-game` stages a pre-accession identity when the recruit does not yet have a Soldier Record.

- Recruits selecting Yes use a Discord member selector; Battalion Clerk validates the selection through the Website before filing it.
- Added final intake questions for referral source and community expectations acknowledgment.
- Added Command-requested Discord intake watcher for adopted Recruiting Cases.
- Manual intake DMs contain a persistent Begin / Resume Intake button and update the existing adopted case rather than creating a duplicate.
