HLLV TELEMETRY RESEARCH PHASE — 22 AUG 2026

Adds research-only evidence capture for exact HLLV role mapping, vehicle/aviation classification and ammunition feasibility research.

New database tables:
- hll_role_mappings
- hll_role_loadout_observations
- hll_research_samples

Linked Soldiers now produce detailed research samples with role ID, observed role label when available, loadout, position, speed, vertical speed, score and combat counters. Public/unlinked players contribute only aggregate role/loadout observations to prevent excessive database growth.

New Discord commands:
- /hll-research — member's latest research sample
- /hll-role-research — staff aggregate role evidence

No automated awards, rank, qualification, MOS certification, flight hours, vehicle occupancy or ammunition expenditure are granted by this research layer.
