PRIMARY DEFECT REPAIRED
The M16 cleaning state could be overwritten by delayed historical Discord/operation round reconciliation. Old firing blocks were inserted at reconciliation time and therefore appeared newer than a recent cleaning. Round events now carry the actual occurrence time. Lifetime rounds always accrue, while rounds-since-cleaning only accrues for rounds fired after last_cleaned_at.

- Historical operation round events use the operation/attendance occurrence time rather than reconciliation time.
