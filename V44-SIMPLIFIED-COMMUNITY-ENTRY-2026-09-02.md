# Battalion Clerk V44 — Simplified Community Entry

## Discord access model
- `Replacement` is the temporary arrival/intake access role.
- `Member` is the normal member-access role after Website-authoritative assignment.
- `NCO` is an authority/access role tied to a current line-leadership billet rather than rank alone.
- Rank roles remain display identity and no longer serve as the basic shared-member access token.
- `5th Cavalry Regiment` remains the broad unit identity/compatibility role.

## Replacement intake area
Battalion Clerk's managed replacement intake category uses a small front door:
- `start-here`
- `recruiting-office`
- `help-desk`
- `replacement-reception` voice

## Messaging
- New-arrival instructions are reduced to Apply → Link → Get Assigned.
- Credential DM is now `ORDERS RECEIVED`, with assignment/MOS/leader when available, roster credentials, Wall Locker/201 File, First 24 Hours, and Report for Duty links.
- First 24 Hours notices replace old Welcome Packet/Command-acceptance language.

## Sync behavior
- Website remains authoritative for rank, MOS, formation, appointments, and membership.
- When a Soldier is assigned, the Clerk removes temporary recruiting roles, adds Member, mirrors active Company/Platoon/Squad roles, and derives NCO access from active leadership billets.
