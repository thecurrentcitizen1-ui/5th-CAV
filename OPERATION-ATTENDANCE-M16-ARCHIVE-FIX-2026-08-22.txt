1/5 CAV — OPERATION ATTENDANCE / M16 / ARCHIVE REPAIR
2026-08-22

FIXED
- Any verified Battalion Clerk OPERATION voice presence now creates/updates an operation history record immediately.
- Presence states: TRACKED PRESENCE -> PARTIAL / LATE -> FULL CREDIT.
- Full credit remains threshold-controlled; partial presence does not inflate credited-operation statistics.
- M16 rounds accrue proportionally to verified attendance and reach the full configured rounds_per_soldier at the full-credit threshold.
- Operation round reconciliation is idempotent; existing rounds are never double-added.
- Historical repair scans existing battalion_event_attendance rows and backfills missing operation participation and M16 round events.
- Historical round repair selects the rifle issued on the operation date when weapon issue history exists, falling back to the current issue only when necessary.
- Ended Clerk operation events are automatically closed and their linked operation becomes COMPLETED.
- On the next calendar day, completed/stale operations become ARCHIVED and no longer appear as scheduled/current.
- Member Current Orders excludes ended/archived operations.
- Member operation history now shows verified partial attendance instead of hiding PARTIAL / LATE rows.
- Added protected Clerk maintenance endpoint for recurring reconciliation.
- Deployment startup runs one idempotent historical repair pass automatically.

RETROACTIVE LIMIT
The repair can only credit historical attendance that exists in Battalion Clerk's persisted attendance ledger. It does not fabricate attendance when no verified historical record exists.
