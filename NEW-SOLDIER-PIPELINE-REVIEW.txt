5TH CAVALRY REGIMENT — NEW SOLDIER PIPELINE REVIEW
2026-08-20

CORRECTED AUTHORITATIVE FLOW
1. Applicant submits Recruiting Case.
2. Discord identity is verified.
3. Battalion Headquarters approves on the WEBSITE.
4. Website immediately opens a PVT / MOS Pending Replacement Detachment 201 File.
5. Soldier appears in Replacement Detachment even with no Company, Platoon, or Squad.
6. S-1 begins and claims in-processing.
7. S-1 completes onboarding.
8. S-1 assigns primary MOS from the controlled catalog.
9. S-1/S-4 issues the M16 from the Soldier quick-action drawer.
10. Soldier acknowledges Standing Orders.
11. S-1/S-3 certifies Initial / Replacement Training.
12. Soldier becomes Ready for Assignment.
13. S-1 files permanent Company/Platoon/Squad or valid HQ/Section assignment.
14. System releases Soldier from Replacement Detachment, closes initial actions,
    files movement orders, closes Recruiting Case as ENLISTED, and queues Discord role mirroring.
15. PVT -> PFC promotion consideration remains separate: 7 days + 1 official Operation
    + completed Replacement Training. No automatic promotion occurs.

ROOT CAUSES FIXED
- A generic unit_node_id was incorrectly treated as a permanent assignment.
- Battalion/root placeholder assignments could hide Soldiers from Replacement Detachment.
- Battalion Clerk required Discord rank/MOS/company/platoon/squad roles before opening a 201 File.
- Recruiting conversion could mark a case ENLISTED before S-1 processing was complete.
- Movement orders could be filed before a true destination existed.
- Replacement Training incorrectly included assignment and PVT promotion gates.
- MOS Pending had no explicit controlled value; 00R Replacement — MOS Pending is now used.
- The Quick File could say ASSIGN MOS without presenting a MOS-only action.

DESIGN SAFEGUARDS
- Website is personnel authority; Discord mirrors the website.
- Provisioning is idempotent and reuses existing personnel/link records.
- S-1 release is gated by Discord link, S-1 onboarding, Standing Orders, MOS,
  M16, training certification, and permanent formation.
- HQ/Section structured assignments are accepted as permanent destinations;
  Battalion/Company/Platoon placeholders are not enough for line personnel.
- Any assignment entry point runs the same release check.
- Final release, not 201 creation, closes the Recruiting Case as ENLISTED.
- No automatic promotion is introduced.
