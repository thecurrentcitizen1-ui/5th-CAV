SITE-WIDE ROSTER READABILITY OVERHAUL — 2026-08-22

Scope
- Battalion personnel index
- Company rosters
- Platoon / squad boards
- Public battalion personnel roster
- Member MY UNIT roster
- Member MY SQUAD roster
- S-1 battalion roster and roster-control list
- Formation identity rosters
- Training qualification roster
- Morning Report roster
- Operation duty/participant roster
- S-1 inactivity/accountability roster
- Generic unit/formation listings

Primary issue found
Several legacy roster components were still rendered at 6–10px on desktop. Some column headers were only 6px, secondary roster details were commonly 7–9px, and multiple responsive rules solved narrow screens by hiding columns rather than presenting the information readably.

Changes
- Standardized normal roster text to approximately 14px.
- Increased Soldier names to approximately 16–17px.
- Increased secondary details to approximately 11–12px.
- Increased roster headers to approximately 11–12px.
- Increased row heights and padding for visual separation.
- Expanded personnel-heavy desktop page widths up to 1680px where appropriate.
- Rebalanced Company and Platoon roster workspace columns to devote more room to personnel.
- Rebuilt MY SQUAD CSS against the current page-my-squad body class.
- Replaced mobile shrink/hide behavior with stacked readable roster-card behavior.
- Preserved backend rank-precedence sorting and all personnel actions/routes.
- Added semantic maintenance classes to formation and operation duty roster components.

No personnel data, authorization rules, rank-precedence logic, or Discord/Battalion Clerk logic was changed.
