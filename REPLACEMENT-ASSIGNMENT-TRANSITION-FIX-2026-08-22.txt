REPLACEMENT DETACHMENT / PERMANENT ASSIGNMENT TRANSITION FIX

- Permanent assignment dropdowns now expose only final duty destinations:
  * Squad
  * Headquarters
  * Section
- Company and Platoon nodes remain organizational containers and can no longer be filed as a Soldier's final permanent assignment through the standard personnel assignment controls.
- process_assignment_action now rejects Company/Platoon/Battalion targets as a defense-in-depth safeguard.
- Replacement Detachment no longer drops Soldiers merely because a Company has been filed.
- A rifle-line Soldier remains in Replacement Detachment until a Squad destination exists.
- Headquarters/Section assignments remain valid without a Squad.
- Partial legacy Company/Platoon assignments remain visible as incomplete and are surfaced as S-1 integrity exceptions.
- Existing final release checklist remains intact; Recruiting Case closure still requires all release gates.
