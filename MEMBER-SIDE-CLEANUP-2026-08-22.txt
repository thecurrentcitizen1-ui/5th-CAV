MEMBER-SIDE CLEANUP — 22 AUG 2026

1. Wall Locker / Footlocker / My Actions
- Removed duplicate rendering of the same NEXT PROMOTION REQUIREMENT in the Action Center.
- Clarified the former WEAPONS DISCIPLINED reputation badge as M16 MAINTAINED with plain-language meaning.

2. Orders Pouch / Battalion Orders
- Member orders are now a read-only failure-isolated route.
- Opening member orders no longer runs staff operation archive/maintenance jobs first.
- Personal orders and relevant published/assigned operations load independently; a single failed source degrades to an empty section rather than Record Processing Error.

3. Operations member tab
- Members now receive a dedicated read-only My Operations view.
- Staff S-3 reconciliation, Clerk repair, scheduling maintenance, and control-board dependencies no longer run before a member can open Operations.
- Current/upcoming and completed/closed operations are separated and show personal duty/attendance/rounds where available.

4. Training
- TRAINING STATUS board font/background contrast corrected for readability.

5. 201 File / MOS Proficiency
- NEXT requirement is now always calculated and displayed from the current proficiency level.
- Live official operation, training-program, and current-duty-qualification counts are populated.
- Fixed Wall Locker MOS display using a legacy list index against a mapping.

6. Award / Training Orders
- Official authority/signature blocks now render uppercase.
- Newly created personnel orders store authority text uppercase as well.

7. Gamertags
- Discord gamertag display formatting removes periods and commas in recruiting/personnel communications selectors and recruiting queues.
