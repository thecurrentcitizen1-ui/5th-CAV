1/5 CAV — Applicant Discord Intake Pipeline — 2026-08-22

Website-authoritative recruiting flow:
1. Applicant connects Discord OAuth (identify + guilds.join).
2. Applicant submits Website application.
3. Battalion Clerk admits the verified Discord identity to the guild before Command approval.
4. Applicant remains a recruiting applicant only; no personnel record, rank, MOS, assignment, Replacement status, or credentials are created pre-approval.
5. Command approves from Website.
6. Website provisions Replacement Detachment 201 File, Battle Roster credentials, and Welcome Packet.
7. Battalion Clerk swaps recruiting status to Replacement Depot and DMs the Battle Roster Number, Field Code, and direct Welcome Packet link.

Existing personnel/assignment/onboarding systems remain authoritative.
