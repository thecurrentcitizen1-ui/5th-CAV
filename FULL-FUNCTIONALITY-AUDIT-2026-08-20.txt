DEFECTS CORRECTED DURING THIS AUDIT
1. Public Battalion sub-pages were blocked by the global access guard.
2. Public endpoints could redirect authenticated staff/members instead of remaining usable.
3. Member Action Center, Journal, Career Goals and unit-history navigation were blocked by scope rules.
4. Training staff had no complete access scope; Training users could be redirected away from their own office.
5. Smart Personnel Search / Compare were visible to staff roles that could not open them.
6. S-1 workload and Training/S-3 workload access were incomplete.
7. S-1 Recruiting buttons were visible even though only Battalion HQ is authorized for Recruiting Control.
8. Training role could see personnel-action navigation but could not use the S-3 training action queue.
9. Training certification/qualification actions did not consistently authorize Training staff.
10. Operations Duplicate and post-operation recommendation controls could be visible to users without execute permission.
11. Operation Detail attendance/round/duty-role controls now become read-only for Training/read-only staff instead of producing 403s.
12. Morning Report POST now has explicit staff-role authorization.
13. Replacement COMPLETE S-1 and batch actions now close/reconcile their workflow actions consistently.
14. Replacement batch Training actions are routed to S-3 instead of S-1.
15. Member ribbon return target is whitelisted to prevent malformed redirect BuildErrors.
16. Headquarters Unit Identity form was accidentally embedded in the HTML title block; moved into the page body.
17. Readiness leadership/cohesion content was accidentally embedded in the title block; moved into the page body.
18. Public Organization personnel rows no longer expose links to private 201 records.
19. Awards, Company and Platoon public pages now fail soft when optional live data is unavailable.
20. Headquarters Unit Identity now receives the unit-node catalog it requires.
21. M16 explanatory copy now matches the implemented model: rounds are primary fouling; prolonged unexcused inactivity can add neglect/environmental fouling.
