1/5 CAV DISCORD ORGANIZATION CLEANUP — SAFETY MODEL

1. Website personnel records remain authoritative.
2. /organization-cleanup with no confirmation is preview-only.
3. /organization-cleanup confirm:True fetches canonical linked personnel first.
4. Role-change echo is suppressed during the migration window.
5. Canonical Company/Platoon/Squad/Team/appointment roles are applied from website data.
6. Duplicate members and channel overwrites are migrated to the surviving canonical role.
7. Duplicate and obsolete generic formation roles are removed only when the Clerk can manage them.
8. Approved channel/category permission blueprints are reapplied.
9. Canonical member roles are reconciled again before role syncing resumes.
10. Any role above the Battalion Clerk in the Discord hierarchy is reported instead of being deleted.
